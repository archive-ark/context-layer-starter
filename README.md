# Context layer starter kit

Six plain files that turn recorded sales calls into something your reps and your AI tools read every time.

This is the template set behind the field guide at https://archiveark.com/field-guide/. Copy it, fill it from five of your own calls, and you have a first version in a week.

## What's here

```
CLAUDE.md                 rules the AI reads first, every session
evidence/transcripts/     your calls, never edited
templates/call-record.md  the five questions, one record per call
knowledge/buyer-pains.md  one pain per entry, counted, quoted
knowledge/objections.md   what they said, what it meant, what worked
foundation/icp.md         who buys, composed from the knowledge files by link
foundation/voice.md       how the founder writes and talks, real samples
log/decisions.md          one line per decision, with the reason
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick five calls.** Two won, two lost, one stalled. Put the transcripts in `evidence/transcripts/` with the outcome on the first line.
2. **Extract.** Copy `templates/call-record.md` once per call and answer the five questions with the buyer's own sentences and timestamps.
3. **Count.** Put the records side by side. Anything said in more than one call goes into `knowledge/` as "k of N calls".
4. **Label.** Every entry carries a status. With five calls almost everything is HYPOTHESIS. That is the honest label.
5. **Use.** Point your AI tool at this folder and run one real task: prep for a call, or a first email to one account. Time it before and after.

## The three rules that make it work

- **Buyer's sentence, never a summary.** With where it was said.
- **A status on every claim.** HYPOTHESIS (a guess), PROXY-EVIDENCE (other people's evidence), VALIDATED (three of your own calls, linked).
- **One fact, one home.** State it once. Everywhere else links to it.

## Limits

Five calls give you per-call facts and a few early counts. They cannot tell you whether a pattern is common, which reply wins, or who your ICP really is. For that you need 15 to 50 calls and a session where the founder marks every pattern confirmed, surprising or wrong.

This kit is a method I run my own agency on. It has not been through a client delivery yet.
