## Where things live
- `evidence/transcripts/` — raw call transcripts. Never edit them.
- `knowledge/buyer-pains.md` — pains, counted, in buyers' words.
- `knowledge/objections.md` — objections, what they meant, what worked.
- `foundation/icp.md` — who buys. Links to the knowledge files; never restates them.
- `foundation/voice.md` — how the founder writes. Match it in every draft.
- `log/decisions.md` — one line per decision, with the reason.

## Rules
1. Every claim carries a status: HYPOTHESIS, PROXY-EVIDENCE or VALIDATED.
   VALIDATED needs three of our own calls, each one linked.
2. Never present a HYPOTHESIS as fact. In a draft to a prospect it may only be asked as a question.
3. Quote buyers word for word, with the transcript and timestamp. No paraphrase.
4. One fact, one home. If a number is already stated in another file, link to it.
5. Never invent a price, a customer name or a result.
6. End every session by updating a file or adding a line to `log/decisions.md`.
